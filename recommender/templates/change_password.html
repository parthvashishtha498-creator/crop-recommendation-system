Password Security Tips

Create a Strong Password

Use at least 8 characters. Combine uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and special symbols.

Keep It Secret

Never share your password with anyone. Our support team will never ask for it.

Change It Regularly

Updating your password every few months helps keep your account secure.

Avoid Common Passwords

Don't use your name, phone number, birth date, or simple passwords like 123456 or password.
